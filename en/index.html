SocialHub CX

Customer service for social media and email in one workspace

SocialHub CX is SocialHub's customer service software for social media and email: comments, direct messages, mentions, reviews and emails become tickets in one shared workspace. An AI Co-Pilot drafts replies from your knowledge base and shows its sources; routing, folders and a first-response target steer the work. SocialHub is a product of maloon GmbH in Ingolstadt, Germany, developed and hosted in Germany.

What SocialHub CX does for your service team

Two modules – Email and Social – with shared ticket logic, AI support and metrics.

Tickets aus E-Mail und Social Media

E-Mails aus Microsoft- oder IMAP-Postfächern, Nachrichten über WhatsApp sowie Kommentare, Direktnachrichten, Erwähnungen, Posts und Bewertungen aus Social Media werden zu Tickets mit Status, Priorität und Zuständigkeit.

Learn more: Tickets aus E-Mail und Social Media

KI-Co-Pilot mit Quellenangabe

Der Co-Pilot entwirft Antworten und fasst Tickets zusammen – auf Basis Ihrer Wissensdatenbank, mit sichtbaren Quellen. Gesendet wird immer von einem Menschen. Die KI von SocialHub CX läuft über Microsoft Azure mit EU-Endpunkt.

Learn more: KI-Co-Pilot mit Quellenangabe

Vorlagen mit Variablen

Antwortvorlagen per „/“ einfügen, in Ordnern organisiert und mit Variablen wie dem Vornamen. Vorlagen pflegen Admins zentral.

Learn more: Vorlagen mit Variablen

Ordner & KI-Tags

Regelbasierte Ordner sortieren die Queue laufend, manuelle Ordner füllen Sie per Drag & Drop. Auf Wunsch verschlagwortet die KI neue Tickets.

Learn more: Ordner & KI-Tags

Routing & Zuweisung

Pro Postfach greifen zuerst Stichwortregeln, dann Team, Person oder Round Robin. Wer auf „Abwesend“ steht, erhält keine neuen Tickets.

Learn more: Routing & Zuweisung

Ziel für die Erstantwort

Legen Sie fest, innerhalb wie vieler Geschäftsstunden die erste Antwort erfolgen soll – das Dashboard zeigt, was pünktlich war und was nicht.

Learn more: Ziel für die Erstantwort

Dashboards & Kennzahlen

Eingang, Rückstand, Erstantwort- und Lösungszeiten, Team- und Agentenleistung; für Social Media Antwortquote, Antwortzeit und Stimmung.

Zusammenarbeit im Team

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is SocialHub CX?

SocialHub CX is customer service software for social media and email. Requests from comments, direct messages, mentions, reviews and mailboxes become tickets in one shared workspace. It adds templates, routing, dashboards and an AI Co-Pilot with sources. The provider is SocialHub, a product of maloon GmbH in Ingolstadt, Germany.

Who is SocialHub CX for?

SocialHub CX is for service and social media teams that answer many written requests and care about data protection. Typical users are transport companies, retailers and brands, loyalty programmes, banks and insurers, municipalities and utilities – especially mid-sized and large organisations and public buyers in the DACH region.

Which channels does SocialHub CX support?

SocialHub CX supports email, WhatsApp and social media. You connect mailboxes via Microsoft (OAuth), Gmail or IMAP/SMTP. The Social module offers Facebook, Instagram, X, YouTube, LinkedIn, Bluesky, TikTok and Threads, plus Google Business Profile, Google Play Store, Trustpilot and WordPress, some of them as add-ons. Web chat is not part of the product.

Where is SocialHub CX data hosted?

SocialHub CX is hosted in Germany (Falkenstein, Nuremberg), like the entire SocialHub platform. Hosting is provided by a provider with a BSI C5 attestation; SocialHub itself does not hold its own C5 attestation. SocialHub is ISO 27001 certified, and a GDPR data processing agreement is available on request.

Does SocialHub CX's AI reply to customers on its own?

No, SocialHub CX does not send AI replies on its own. The Co-Pilot creates drafts and summaries, but a person reviews and sends every reply. Even automatically prepared suggestions are never sent by themselves. The Co-Pilot shows the sources from your knowledge base so your team can check statements quickly.

Where does SocialHub CX's AI run?

SocialHub CX's AI runs on Microsoft Azure with an EU endpoint. The Co-Pilot drafts replies and summaries based on your knowledge base and shows its sources. A person sends every reply. SocialHub CX data itself is hosted in Germany (Falkenstein, Nuremberg).

How does SocialHub CX differ from Sprinklr or Zendesk?

SocialHub CX focuses on social media and email and comes from a German provider hosting in Germany. Sprinklr is a unified CXM platform for large enterprises, Zendesk a broad service system with many channels and add-ons. Which fits depends on your channels, team size and compliance requirements.

How can I get to know SocialHub CX?

The best way to get to know SocialHub CX is a demo. We show you the workspace with examples from your industry and address your channels and requirements. For a start in live operation we offer a pilot; for procurement and IT we provide documents.
