SocialHub CX

One platform to manage customer experience across all channels

SocialHub CX is SocialHub's customer service software for social media, WhatsApp, email and website chat: comments, direct messages, mentions, reviews and emails become tickets in one shared workspace. An AI Co-Pilot drafts replies from your knowledge base and past conversations and shows its sources; routing, folders and a first-response target steer the work. SocialHub is a product of maloon GmbH in Ingolstadt, Germany, developed and hosted in Germany.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is SocialHub CX?

SocialHub CX is customer service software for social media, WhatsApp, email and website chat. Requests from comments, direct messages, mentions, reviews and mailboxes become tickets in one shared workspace. It adds templates, routing, dashboards and an AI Co-Pilot with sources. The provider is SocialHub, a product of maloon GmbH in Ingolstadt, Germany.

Who is SocialHub CX for?

SocialHub CX is for service and social media teams that answer many written requests and care about data protection. Typical users are transport companies, retailers and brands, loyalty programmes, banks and insurers, municipalities and utilities – especially mid-sized and large organisations and public buyers in the DACH region.

Which channels does SocialHub CX support?

SocialHub CX supports email, WhatsApp, website chat and social media. You connect mailboxes via Microsoft (OAuth), Gmail or IMAP/SMTP. The Social module offers Facebook, Instagram, X, YouTube, LinkedIn, Bluesky, TikTok and Threads, plus Google Business Profile, Google Play Store, Trustpilot and WordPress, some of them as add-ons.

Where is SocialHub CX data hosted?

SocialHub CX is hosted in Germany (Falkenstein, Nuremberg), like the entire SocialHub platform. Hosting is provided by a provider with a BSI C5 attestation; SocialHub itself does not hold its own C5 attestation. SocialHub is ISO 27001 certified, and a GDPR data processing agreement is available on request.

Does SocialHub CX's AI reply to customers on its own?

No, SocialHub CX does not send AI replies on its own. The Co-Pilot creates drafts and summaries, but a person reviews and sends every reply. Even automatically prepared suggestions are never sent by themselves. The Co-Pilot shows the sources of its drafts from your knowledge base and past conversations so your team can check statements quickly.

Where does SocialHub CX's AI run?

SocialHub CX's AI runs on Microsoft Azure with an EU endpoint. The Co-Pilot drafts replies and summaries based on your knowledge base and past conversations and shows its sources. A person sends every reply. SocialHub CX data itself is hosted in Germany (Falkenstein, Nuremberg).

How does SocialHub CX differ from Sprinklr or Zendesk?

SocialHub CX focuses on social media, WhatsApp, email and website chat and comes from a German provider hosting in Germany. Sprinklr is a unified CXM platform for large enterprises, Zendesk a broad service system with many channels and add-ons. Which fits depends on your channels, team size and compliance requirements.

How can I get to know SocialHub CX?

The best way to get to know SocialHub CX is a demo. We show you the workspace with examples from your industry and address your channels and requirements. For a start in live operation we offer a pilot; for procurement and IT we provide documents.
