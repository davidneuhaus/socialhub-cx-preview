Email ticketing

Email ticketing: handle service mailboxes as tickets

SocialHub CX turns every incoming email into a ticket with ticket number, status, priority and owner – with routing, templates and signatures per mailbox.

Request a demoProcurement & IT pack

What it's about

In the Email module your team works with your existing service mailboxes. Every incoming email becomes a ticket with a ticket number; replies in the thread are assigned to the ticket. Each mailbox has its own settings for routing, signature and automatic acknowledgement.

What's included

  • Connect mailboxes via Microsoft (OAuth), Gmail or IMAP/SMTP; “Sync now” fetches new messages immediately
  • Tickets with status, priority, owner and tags
  • Views such as Priority, My tickets, Unassigned, Drafts and Snoozed
  • Template folders, e.g. by language or topic
  • Variables such as {{customer.firstName}} or {{ticket.number}}
  • Link help-centre articles, with suggestions matching the ticket
  • Signatures per mailbox, applied automatically
  • Automatic acknowledgement per mailbox with ticket number
  • Held-back emails, e.g. undeliverable or out-of-office, with reason – one click to the ticket

How your team works with it

  1. A new ticket arrives and is assigned to the right team by keyword rule.
  2. The agent opens the ticket and sees customer history and internal notes.
  3. She inserts a template with “/” or lets the Co-Pilot prepare a draft with sources.
  4. She reviews, adds and sends – the mailbox signature is applied automatically.
  5. The ticket is solved; the dashboard shows whether the first-response target was met.

What's not included

Not included are web chat, CRM integrations and an open API.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which mailboxes can I connect to SocialHub CX?

SocialHub CX connects mailboxes via Microsoft (OAuth), Gmail or via IMAP/SMTP, so Microsoft 365 mailboxes as well as other email servers. Your email address stays the same. Per mailbox you set routing, signature and automatic acknowledgement, and “Sync now” fetches new messages immediately when needed.

Do customers receive an acknowledgement from SocialHub CX?

Yes, SocialHub CX sends an automatic acknowledgement with ticket number per mailbox if you want it. You define the text yourself, for example with notes on processing times. Replies to this acknowledgement are assigned to the same ticket, so the history stays complete.

How does SocialHub CX prevent two people from answering the same ticket?

SocialHub CX gives every ticket a clear owner, either by routing rule, round robin or manually. The “My tickets” and “Unassigned” views show who is working on what. The activity log records who replied or changed the owner and when.

Q&A

Questions and answers

How easy is it to connect an existing email mailbox?

SocialHub CX connects existing mailboxes via Microsoft (OAuth) or IMAP/SMTP, without you changing your email address. Per mailbox you set routing, signature and automatic acknowledgement. SocialHub CX fetches new messages continuously; with “Sync now” it happens immediately at the push of a button.

How do signatures per mailbox work?

SocialHub CX stores signatures per mailbox and applies them automatically when replying. This way, replies from the “Subscriptions” mailbox carry a different signature than replies from “Complaints”, without staff having to remember. In addition, an automatic acknowledgement with ticket number can be set up per mailbox.
