Features

SocialHub CX features

SocialHub CX combines email ticketing, a Social module, an AI Co-Pilot with sources, templates, routing, a first-response target and dashboards for your social media and email customer service.

Tickets aus E-Mail und Social Media

Two modules, one workspace: the Email module works with service mailboxes, the Social module with your social media profiles. Both are tickets with the same core concepts.

  • Connect mailboxes via Microsoft (OAuth) or IMAP/SMTP, sync on demand
  • Social channels: Facebook, Instagram, X, YouTube, LinkedIn, Bluesky, TikTok and Threads, plus Google Business Profile, Google Play Store, Trustpilot and WordPress – some channels as add-ons
  • WhatsApp as a channel
  • Message types in the Social module: comments, direct messages, mentions, posts, reviews
  • Eleven views in the Email module, including Priority, My tickets, Drafts, Snoozed, Waiting
  • Search by ticket number, person or content; filter by channel, tag, priority, status, assignee and date range
  • Bulk actions (done, assign to me, priority), snooze until a set time, merge tickets
  • Held emails (e.g. bounces, out-of-office replies) with reason – convert to a ticket in one click

KI-Co-Pilot mit Quellenangabe

The Co-Pilot works with what you approve: help-centre articles, files and – if you switch it on – solved tickets. It always shows what its answer is based on.

  • Quick actions in a ticket: “Draft a reply”, “Summarise ticket”, “What does the knowledge base say?” (email tickets)
  • General chat with history; ask in any language
  • Optional: prepare suggested replies automatically – never sent automatically, one reply per customer message
  • Optional: learn from solved tickets of the last 12 months, as anonymised summaries
  • Precedence rule: if an article and a learned ticket disagree, the article wins
  • Feedback loop with ratings, review queue and CSV export
  • Knowledge sources with title and semantic search and a status per source
  • SocialHub CX's AI runs on Microsoft Azure with an EU endpoint.

Vorlagen mit Variablen

Recurring questions deserve good, consistent answers. Templates are available with “/” in the editor and in internal notes.

  • Template folders, e.g. by language or topic
  • Variables such as {{customer.firstName}} or {{ticket.number}}
  • Link help-centre articles, with suggestions matching the ticket
  • Signatures per mailbox, applied automatically
  • Automatic acknowledgement per mailbox with ticket number
Screenshot: Vorlagen mit Variablen

Ordner & KI-Tags

Folders answer questions about the queue, such as “everything waiting on customers for a week” – and never show more than someone is allowed to see anyway.

  • Rule-based folders that keep updating
  • Manual folders via drag & drop
  • Shared account-wide, created by admins
  • Tags for topics; optional AI tagging of new tickets
  • AI can improve tag descriptions on request – always reversible
Screenshot: Ordner & KI-Tags

Routing & Zuweisung

New email tickets land where they belong – without anyone sorting by hand.

  • Per mailbox: keyword rules first
  • Then to a team, a person or round robin – or deliberately unassigned
  • Online/Away status controls who receives new tickets
  • Manual assignment in list and ticket; bulk action “Assign to me”
  • Teams as their own views and assignment targets

Dashboards & Kennzahlen

See how your service is running – and who did what.

  • Email: total, solved, unsolved tickets, median first-response time, late first responses, resolution time, one-touch rate, reopens
  • Trends, backlog, arrival times by hour, weekday or month, team performance

What is not part of the product

SocialHub CX is designed for social media, WhatsApp and email. Not included are web chat, CRM integrations and an open API. As its SLA metric, SocialHub CX measures the first-response target. If one of these points is decisive for you, we'll tell you openly whether SocialHub CX fits.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I connect Microsoft 365 mailboxes to SocialHub CX?

Yes, SocialHub CX connects Microsoft 365 mailboxes via Microsoft OAuth; other mailboxes connect via IMAP/SMTP. Each mailbox has its own settings for routing, signature and automatic acknowledgement. With “Sync now” you fetch new messages immediately when needed.

How does SocialHub CX distribute new tickets?

SocialHub CX distributes new email tickets per mailbox using keyword rules first. If no rule matches, they go to a team, a person, round robin, or stay deliberately unassigned. Staff set to “Away” receive no new tickets; manual assignment is possible at any time.

Can SocialHub CX measure service levels?

Yes, SocialHub CX measures a first-response target, counted in business hours and selectable from one to 24 hours or custom. The dashboard shows on-time and late first responses per team and person. Other SLA types such as resolution targets or escalation rules are not part of the product.

Where does SocialHub CX's Co-Pilot get its knowledge?

SocialHub CX's Co-Pilot uses the ticket subject and latest messages, matching help-centre articles and files, up to three similar solved tickets and the chat history so far. It shows the sources of its answer. If an article and a learned ticket disagree, the article wins.

Does SocialHub CX use solved tickets for the AI?

SocialHub CX only uses solved tickets if you switch on “Learn from solved tickets”; it is off by default. Then tickets from the last twelve months serve as anonymised summaries. Content your team repeatedly rates as wrong lands in a review queue and is removed.

Which metrics does SocialHub CX show?

SocialHub CX's email dashboard shows total, solved and unsolved tickets, median first-response time, late first responses, resolution time, one-touch rate and reopens, among others. The social dashboard shows response rate, response and handling time and the sentiment of incoming messages by channel and type.
