AI Co-Pilot

AI Co-Pilot with sources – people decide

SocialHub CX's Co-Pilot drafts replies and summaries from the sources you approve and shows them. A person sends every reply.

Request a demoProcurement & IT pack

What it's about

The Co-Pilot works with what you approve: help-centre articles, files and – if you switch it on – solved tickets. It always shows what its answer is based on. SocialHub CX's AI runs on Microsoft Azure with an EU endpoint. SocialHub CX does not train AI models on customer data.

What's included

  • Quick actions in email and social tickets: “Draft a reply”, “Summarise ticket”, “What does the knowledge base say?”
  • General chat with history; ask in any language
  • Optional: prepare suggested replies automatically – never sent automatically, one reply per customer message
  • Optional: learn from solved tickets of the last 12 months, as anonymised summaries
  • Precedence rule: if an article and a learned ticket disagree, the article wins
  • Feedback loop with ratings, review queue and CSV export
  • Knowledge sources with title and semantic search and a status per source
  • SocialHub CX's AI runs on Microsoft Azure with an EU endpoint.

How your team works with it

  1. Your team opens a ticket and chooses “Draft a reply”.
  2. The Co-Pilot uses the ticket content, matching help-centre articles, files and – if enabled – similar solved tickets.
  3. The draft shows its sources; your team reviews, edits and sends.
  4. Your team rates the answer; content repeatedly rated as wrong lands in a review queue.

What's not included

The Co-Pilot does not send replies on its own, and prepared drafts are never sent automatically. A chatbot for customers is not part of the product.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does SocialHub CX's Co-Pilot send replies on its own?

No, SocialHub CX's Co-Pilot does not send replies on its own. It creates drafts and summaries, and a person reviews and sends every reply. Even automatically prepared suggestions are never sent automatically; there is at most one suggestion per customer message.

Where does SocialHub CX's AI run?

SocialHub CX's AI runs on Microsoft Azure with an EU endpoint. The Co-Pilot uses your approved knowledge sources and shows them with every answer. SocialHub CX does not train AI models on customer data. SocialHub CX's other data is hosted in Germany (Falkenstein, Nuremberg).

Does SocialHub CX use solved tickets for the AI?

SocialHub CX only uses solved tickets if you switch on the option “Learn from solved tickets”; it is off by default. Then tickets from the last twelve months serve as anonymised summaries. If an article and a learned ticket disagree, the article wins. SocialHub CX does not train AI models on customer data.

Q&A

Questions and answers

Where does the AI get its answers, and how do I prevent made-up content?

SocialHub CX lets the Co-Pilot answer only from sources you approve and shows those sources with every answer. These include help-centre articles, files and optionally solved tickets. If an article and a learned ticket disagree, the article wins. Your team checks every draft before sending, and content rated as wrong lands in a review queue.

How do I make sure sensitive replies, e.g. on refunds, data protection or legal matters, are approved by a person?

In SocialHub CX a person always approves every reply, because the Co-Pilot sends nothing itself. It creates drafts and summaries; even automatically prepared suggestions are never sent automatically. A multi-step approval workflow with a second person is not part of the product. For sensitive topics, aligned templates, internal notes and assignment to experienced colleagues help.

Does the AI pull content from unsuitable articles, and how do I correct that?

SocialHub CX shows the sources used with every Co-Pilot answer, so your team spots unsuitable articles immediately. Your team rates answers; learned content repeatedly rated as wrong lands in a review queue and is removed. You correct the article itself in the knowledge base, because articles take precedence over learned tickets.

Does the AI learn from old tickets, including bad replies?

SocialHub CX only learns from solved tickets if you switch on the option “Learn from solved tickets”; it is off by default. Then tickets from the last twelve months serve as anonymised summaries. The feedback loop catches bad content: entries repeatedly rated as wrong are reviewed and removed. SocialHub CX does not train AI models on customer data.

How do agents rate AI answers in the ticket, and how is that fed back?

In SocialHub CX agents rate Co-Pilot answers directly in the ticket, and these ratings steer knowledge quality. Content repeatedly rated as wrong lands in a review queue and is removed. Ratings can be exported as CSV. SocialHub CX does not train AI models on customer data.

Does SocialHub CX use AI, and for what?

Yes, SocialHub CX uses AI in the Co-Pilot, which creates draft replies, summaries and answers to internal questions from your approved knowledge sources and shows the sources. On request, AI assigns tags automatically. A person sends every reply. SocialHub CX's AI runs on Microsoft Azure with an EU endpoint. SocialHub CX does not train AI models on customer data.

Can AI draft replies from our documents without sending them itself?

Yes, SocialHub CX's Co-Pilot drafts replies from your approved help-centre articles and files and never sends on its own. It shows the sources with every draft, and your team reviews, edits and sends. Even automatically prepared suggestions are never sent automatically. SocialHub CX's AI runs on Microsoft Azure with an EU endpoint.

How do we keep AI answers in support accurate?

SocialHub CX keeps Co-Pilot answers accurate by using only approved sources, showing them and letting a person send every reply. Articles take precedence over learned tickets. Your team rates answers; content repeatedly rated as wrong lands in a review queue and is removed. Up-to-date help-centre articles are therefore the most important lever.

Can AI help with the same recurring questions from our documents?

Yes, SocialHub CX helps with recurring questions through a Co-Pilot that drafts replies from your help-centre articles and files and shows its sources. In addition, your team inserts aligned templates with variables using “/”. Optionally, the Co-Pilot learns from solved tickets of the last twelve months as anonymised summaries; your team always sends.

How much automation makes sense in support, and where does the person stay involved?

SocialHub CX automates distribution and preparation, while a person decides on and sends every reply. Keyword rules and round robin distribute tickets, AI assigns tags on request, and the Co-Pilot prepares drafts and summaries. There are no automatically sent replies, so all external communication stays in your team's hands.

What to do when a support bot doesn't help with points questions?

SocialHub CX doesn't rely on a customer-facing bot but on a Co-Pilot for your team: a person answers every request. The Co-Pilot drafts replies from your programme terms and shows its sources. Members asking about points get a checked answer instead of a bot loop.

Why does an AI assistant give wrong loyalty terms, and how do you prevent it?

SocialHub CX reduces the risk of wrong answers on loyalty terms because the Co-Pilot only uses approved sources and shows them; articles take precedence over learned tickets. Wrong answers often come from outdated or contradictory sources. Your team checks every draft and rates answers, and learned content repeatedly rated as wrong lands in a review queue.
