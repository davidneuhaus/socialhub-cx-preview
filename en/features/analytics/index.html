Analytics

Analytics: see how your service is running

SocialHub CX shows in dashboards for email and social media how fast your team replies, where requests pile up and how the Co-Pilot is used.

Request a demoProcurement & IT pack

What it's about

See how your service is running – and who did what.

What's included

  • Email: total, solved, unsolved tickets, median first-response time, late first responses, resolution time, one-touch rate, reopens
  • Trends, backlog, arrival times by hour, weekday or month, team performance
  • Compare with previous period or previous year; filter by team and person
  • Agent table and Co-Pilot metrics
  • Social: tickets, handled, response rate, response time, handling time, breakdown by type, channel and sentiment
  • Dashboard: late first responses, on time vs. late per team and person

How your team works with it

  1. Choose date range, team or person.
  2. Compare with the previous period or year.
  3. Check backlog, arrival times and late first responses.
  4. Derive actions, such as new keyword rules or templates.

What's not included

The dashboards are separate for email and social media and can be filtered by date range, team and person.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which metrics does SocialHub CX show?

SocialHub CX shows for email, among others, total, solved and unsolved tickets, median first-response time, late first responses, resolution time, one-touch rate and reopens. The social dashboard shows tickets, response rate, response time, handling time and the breakdown by type, channel and sentiment.

Can I compare periods in SocialHub CX?

Yes, SocialHub CX compares every selected period with the previous period or year. Filters by team and person show where things get stuck. Trends, backlog and arrival times by hour, weekday or month help with shift planning and preparing for peaks.

Does SocialHub CX measure how the Co-Pilot is used?

Yes, SocialHub CX shows Co-Pilot metrics: replies with Co-Pilot, accepted drafts and answers rated helpful. Your team's ratings are also available in a feedback overview with CSV export. This shows whether your knowledge sources hold up.

Q&A

Questions and answers

How do I categorise and analyse complaints from social media?

SocialHub CX categorises complaints via tags your team sets or AI assigns automatically on request, and analyses them in dashboards. Rule-based folders group topics continuously. The social dashboard shows tickets, response rate, response time and the breakdown by type, channel and sentiment. In the email dashboard you compare periods with the previous period or year.

How do I measure whether AI suggestions are used and helpful?

SocialHub CX shows Co-Pilot metrics in the dashboard: replies with Co-Pilot, accepted drafts and answers rated helpful. In addition, your team rates individual answers; ratings are available in a feedback overview with CSV export. Content repeatedly rated as wrong lands in a review queue.

Which metrics should a social team measure at high comment volumes?

SocialHub CX provides the key metrics for social teams in the social dashboard: tickets, handled tickets, response rate, response time, handling time and the breakdown by type, channel and sentiment. In the social dashboard you choose the date range; comparisons by team and person and with the previous period are offered by the email dashboard.
