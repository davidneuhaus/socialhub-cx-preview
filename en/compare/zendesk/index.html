SocialHub CX vs Zendesk

SocialHub CX vs. Zendesk

SocialHub CX is customer service software for social media, WhatsApp, email and website chat from a German provider. Zendesk is a broad service system from a US provider with many channels and add-ons. Here are the sourced facts side by side.

In short

  • SocialHub CX turns comments, direct messages, mentions and reviews plus emails into tickets in one workspace.
  • According to its help centre, Zendesk does not create tickets from Instagram comments and does not offer TikTok in-product.
  • Zendesk offers EEA hosting (Ireland or Frankfurt) from Suite Professional at no extra cost, once activated; there is no commitment to Germany only.
  • SocialHub hosts in Germany (Falkenstein, Nuremberg).

When Zendesk is the better choice

  • You need a broad service system with chat, messaging, help centre and many marketplace apps.
  • You want channels such as WeChat, LINE or Apple Messages for Business.
  • You work internationally and need hosting regions outside Europe.
  • You need deep integrations with CRM and shop systems.

When SocialHub CX fits

  • Instagram comments, TikTok and reviews should become tickets in service.
  • Social media, WhatsApp, email and website chat should be handled in one workspace.
  • You prefer a German provider hosting in Germany.
  • You want a social dashboard with sentiment without an additional module.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is SocialHub CX an alternative to Zendesk?

SocialHub CX is an alternative to Zendesk for teams whose service mainly runs via social media, WhatsApp, email and website chat. SocialHub CX also turns Instagram comments, TikTok messages and reviews into tickets. Zendesk is broader, for example with chat, help centre and many integrations.

Does Zendesk turn Instagram comments into tickets?

SocialHub CX turns Instagram comments into tickets; according to its own help centre, Zendesk does not. At Zendesk, Instagram direct messages and story replies become tickets, and “Tickets are not created for comments or replies on posts, reels, or other Instagram content” (as of 20 Oct 2026). Zendesk does not offer TikTok in-product.

Does Zendesk offer hosting in Germany?

SocialHub CX is hosted in Germany (Falkenstein, Nuremberg); Zendesk offers the EEA region with data centres in Ireland or Frankfurt. According to Zendesk, the “Data Center Location” option is included at no extra cost from Suite Professional but must be activated. A specific country cannot be selected (as of 20 Oct 2026).

What does SocialHub CX cost compared with Zendesk?

SocialHub CX has no public price; you receive an individual quote. Zendesk publishes list prices, with add-ons on top. It is best to compare quotes for the same channels, features and seats, including all required add-ons.

Do SocialHub CX and Zendesk process AI in the EU?

SocialHub CX's AI runs on Microsoft Azure with an EU endpoint. Zendesk states that the infrastructure for generative replies of its AI agents is located in the EU (as of 20 Oct 2026). Both providers therefore offer EU processing for the AI features mentioned.
