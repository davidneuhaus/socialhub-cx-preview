SocialHub CX vs Swat.io

SocialHub CX vs. Swat.io

SocialHub CX is customer service software for social media, WhatsApp and email by SocialHub from Germany. Swat.io is a social media management tool from Austria with an inbox add-on. Here are the sourced facts side by side.

In short

  • SocialHub CX is designed for customer service: social media, WhatsApp and email as tickets with routing, first-response target and AI Co-Pilot.
  • Swat.io is a social media management tool with planning, inbox add-on and insights; the “Support Email Inbox” is listed as an integration.
  • Both providers come from the DACH region, are ISO 27001 certified and offer a DPA.
  • SocialHub CX: hosted in Germany (Falkenstein, Nuremberg) by a provider with a BSI C5 attestation; SocialHub itself does not hold its own C5 attestation.

When Swat.io is the better choice

  • You mainly need a tool for social media planning, approvals and reporting with an optional inbox.
  • You want to see prices online and test the tool yourself.
  • You need Pinterest or a documented DORA addendum or a published uptime SLA.
  • Your team is small and doesn't need email service with routing per mailbox.

When SocialHub CX fits

  • Your customer service handles social media, WhatsApp and email together as tickets.
  • You need routing per mailbox, a first-response target and dashboards for email and social media.
  • You want an AI Co-Pilot that shows sources from your knowledge base.
  • You want hosting in Germany (Falkenstein, Nuremberg) by a provider with a BSI C5 attestation.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is SocialHub CX an alternative to Swat.io?

SocialHub CX is an alternative to Swat.io for teams handling customer service via social media, WhatsApp and email together. SocialHub CX offers email ticketing with routing, a first-response target and an AI Co-Pilot with sources. Swat.io focuses on social media management with an optional inbox.

Does SocialHub have a BSI C5 attestation?

SocialHub CX and SocialHub do not hold their own BSI C5 attestation. Hosting is in Germany (Falkenstein, Nuremberg), by a provider with a BSI C5 attestation. SocialHub itself is ISO 27001 certified. Further hosting information is available on request.

Where do SocialHub CX and Swat.io host?

SocialHub CX is hosted in Germany (Falkenstein, Nuremberg). Swat.io states “EU-Hosting” and “Serverstandort in DE” on its website (as of 20 Oct 2026). Both providers are ISO 27001 certified and offer a data processing agreement under GDPR.

Can I handle emails with SocialHub CX and Swat.io?

SocialHub CX has its own Email module: mailboxes via Microsoft, Gmail or IMAP/SMTP, routing per mailbox, templates, signatures and a first-response target. Swat.io lists a “Support Email Inbox” among its integrations (as of 20 Oct 2026). It is best to clarify the exact scope directly with each provider.

What does SocialHub CX cost compared with Swat.io?

SocialHub CX has no public price; you receive an individual quote. Swat.io publishes its prices, with the inbox as an add-on. It is best to compare costs for the same channels, user numbers and features, including inbox and AI.
