SocialHub CX vs Sprinklr

SocialHub CX vs. Sprinklr Service

SocialHub CX is customer service software for social media, WhatsApp, email and website chat from a German provider. Sprinklr Service is part of a unified CXM platform for large enterprises. Here are the sourced facts side by side.

In short

  • SocialHub CX focuses on social media, WhatsApp, email and website chat as tickets in one workspace.
  • Sprinklr Service is part of a unified CXM platform with, by its own account, more than 30 channels and many service modules.
  • SocialHub hosts in Germany (Falkenstein, Nuremberg); Sprinklr offers hosting in Germany, and US hosting applies if nothing else is agreed.
  • Sprinklr does not publish prices; self-serve was discontinued as of 30 April 2026.

When Sprinklr is the better choice

  • You need one platform for marketing, social listening, service and further channels.
  • You run a large customer service operation with many service modules, e.g. workforce and quality management.
  • You need channels SocialHub CX does not cover.
  • You are looking for a provider with global presence and many enterprise references.

When SocialHub CX fits

  • You mainly handle social media, WhatsApp, email and website chat and want a focused workspace for it.
  • You prefer a German provider hosting in Germany.
  • You want an AI Co-Pilot with sources where a person always sends.
  • You are a mid-sized organisation or public body and want procurement & IT documents tailored to your request.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is SocialHub CX an alternative to Sprinklr?

SocialHub CX is an alternative to Sprinklr for teams that mainly handle social media, WhatsApp, email and website chat in customer service. Sprinklr Service is part of a broad unified CXM platform with many modules. If you don't need that scope, SocialHub CX offers a focused workspace from a German provider.

Where does Sprinklr host, and where does SocialHub CX host?

SocialHub CX is hosted in Germany (Falkenstein, Nuremberg). By its own account, Sprinklr hosts in data centres in the US and Europe; Hosting in Germany via Google Cloud has been available since 18 March 2025. Without a different agreement, US hosting applies at Sprinklr (as of 20 Oct 2026).

What does Sprinklr cost compared with SocialHub CX?

SocialHub CX has no public price; you receive an individual quote. Sprinklr does not publish prices either and discontinued self-serve as of 30 April 2026 (as of 20 Oct 2026). For a comparison, it is worth requesting quotes from both providers for the same channels and team size.

Where do SocialHub CX and Sprinklr process AI requests?

SocialHub CX's AI runs on Microsoft Azure with an EU endpoint. Sprinklr's sub-processor list names “Azure OpenAI – US for US customers, EU for EU customers” and “OpenAI, LLC – US” for AI+ features (as of October 2026). Which features you use at Sprinklr determines the respective sub-processor.

Does Sprinklr have a BSI C5 attestation, and does SocialHub CX?

SocialHub CX does not hold its own BSI C5 attestation; hosting is provided by a provider with a BSI C5 attestation. Sprinklr lists C5 in its trust portal (vendor statement). According to the BSI, a C5 attestation always applies to specific cloud services. Ask both providers for the report covering the services you use.
