FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is SocialHub CX suitable for banks and insurers?

SocialHub CX is suitable for banks and insurers that want to handle social media and email requests in a structured way. SocialHub is a German provider with hosting in Germany (Falkenstein, Nuremberg) and ISO 27001 certification; a DPA is available on request. Whether SocialHub CX meets your regulatory requirements is for you to assess.

Does SocialHub CX support our DORA review?

SocialHub CX supports your vendor review with the service description (available online), SocialHub's ISO 27001 certificate and further documents for data-protection, security and suitability reviews, tailored to your request. DORA obligations lie with your institution; we do not claim DORA compliance of our own.

Can we trace in SocialHub CX who replied what?

Yes, SocialHub CX keeps an activity log per ticket: who changed status, owner or tags and who replied, and when. Internal notes and the customer history show the course across several tickets. A person always reviews and sends AI drafts.

Does SocialHub CX support single sign-on?

Yes, SocialHub CX supports single sign-on (SSO). Your staff sign in through your organisation's central login, and accounts are managed via the SocialHub platform. We clarify connection details with your IT.

Q&A

Questions and answers

How long do bank customers wait for written replies?

SocialHub CX shows banks and insurers their own waiting time for written replies instead of relying on industry figures. You set a first-response target in business hours, and the dashboard shows median first-response time, late first responses and resolution time per team. Keyword rules route emails directly to departments such as accounts, cards or claims.

How quickly do direct banks reply by email?

SocialHub CX measures how quickly your institution replies by email: the dashboard shows median first-response time, late first responses, resolution time and one-touch rate, compared with the previous period. A first-response target in business hours makes delays visible. We don't quote industry figures; your own value is what counts.

How do customers experience a direct bank's customer service?

SocialHub CX helps direct banks make written customer service more reliable: requests from social media and service mailboxes become tickets with clear ownership and a first-response target. Customers therefore get consistent replies based on aligned templates. How satisfied your customers are is something you measure with your own surveys.
