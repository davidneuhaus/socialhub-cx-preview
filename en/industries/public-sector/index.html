Public sector

Citizen service for public administration: handle social media and email centrally

SocialHub CX captures citizen requests from social media and email centrally as tickets, distributes them to departments and teams, and helps with consistent replies through templates and an AI Co-Pilot. SocialHub is a German provider hosting in Germany, ISO 27001 and single sign-on.

Typical challenges

  • Requests arrive via Facebook, Instagram, shared mailboxes and other channels – often another department is responsible.
  • The communications office answers a lot but needs input from departments.
  • Procurement, IT security and data protection require reliable evidence before a tool can be introduced.
  • Administrative replies must be factually correct and consistent.

How SocialHub CX helps

Capture centrally

Social media messages, WhatsApp messages and shared mailboxes as tickets in one workspace.

Involve departments

Reply consistently

Templates with variables; Co-Pilot drafts with sources from your citizen information.

Measure first response

Access

Single sign-on (SSO); folders never show more than a person may see.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does SocialHub CX support public procurement?

SocialHub CX supports your procurement with a published service description, SocialHub's ISO 27001 certificate and further documents for data-protection, security and suitability reviews, tailored to your request. This helps you prepare market research, suitability checks and specifications.

Can SocialHub CX distribute requests to different departments?

Yes, SocialHub CX distributes requests to teams you create, for example one per department. Keyword rules per mailbox assign emails automatically; social media tickets you assign manually. Internal notes and watchers involve departments without citizens seeing the coordination.

Is SocialHub CX accessible?

SocialHub CX has been tested for accessibility according to BITV. This lets you assess the requirements of BITV 2.0 and EN 301 549 in your procurement. Further accessibility information is available on request.

Where is SocialHub CX data hosted for municipalities?

SocialHub CX is hosted in Germany (Falkenstein, Nuremberg) for all customers. Hosting is provided by a provider with a BSI C5 attestation; SocialHub itself does not hold its own C5 attestation. SocialHub is ISO 27001 certified, and a DPA under Art. 28 GDPR is available on request.

Q&A

Questions and answers

Do citizens need other channels besides social media to contact public bodies?

SocialHub CX helps public bodies run social media as one of several channels, because email mailboxes and social media profiles run in the same workspace. Citizens can write by email, WhatsApp or social media, and your team handles everything by the same rules. Which channels your authority must offer legally is something to clarify with your legal or data protection office.

Does BITV 2.0 also apply to federal states and municipalities?

SocialHub CX has been tested for accessibility according to BITV. BITV 2.0 applies to federal public bodies; federal states and municipalities have their own state rules, which usually build on the same requirements under EN 301 549. Which rule applies to your municipality is clarified by your contracting authority.

What response time do citizens expect in public comment sections?

There is no fixed expectation, but SocialHub CX makes your own response time visible: the social dashboard shows response rate and response time for comments and messages. For emails, you also set a first-response target in business hours, such as 1, 2, 4, 8 or 24 hours. Which target suits your citizens is decided by your authority.
