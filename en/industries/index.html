What all industries have in common

  • AI Co-Pilot with sources; a person sends every reply

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which industries is SocialHub CX suitable for?

SocialHub CX suits service teams with many requests via social media, WhatsApp, email and website chat, especially in public transport, retail and loyalty, banking and insurance, public administration and utilities. What these teams share is that several mailboxes and profiles must be handled with clear ownership.
