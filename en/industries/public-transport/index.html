FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does SocialHub CX help transport operators during disruptions?

SocialHub CX brings all requests from social media, WhatsApp, email and website chat together as tickets in one workspace during disruptions. Keyword rules route them to the right team, aligned templates ensure consistent replies, and rule-based folders group similar requests so your team handles peaks in a structured way.

Can SocialHub CX assign Deutschlandticket requests automatically?

Yes, SocialHub CX assigns email requests automatically via keyword rules per mailbox, for terms such as “Deutschlandticket”, “cancellation” or “refund”. They then go to a team, a person or round robin to available staff. Optionally, AI tags new tickets so you can analyse topics in the dashboard later.

Can SocialHub CX's Co-Pilot answer fare and timetable questions?

SocialHub CX's Co-Pilot drafts replies based on the help-centre articles and files you provide and on past conversations, such as fare conditions or refund rules. It shows the sources so your team can check statements. A person sends every reply; SocialHub CX does not connect live timetable data.

Is SocialHub CX suitable for tenders by transport operators?

SocialHub CX is suitable for tenders on social media and email customer dialogue, for example passenger requests and complaint management. For suitability checks you receive the service description (available online), SocialHub's ISO 27001 certificate and further documents for data-protection, security and suitability reviews, tailored to your request. SocialHub CX does not cover tenders focused on other channels.

Q&A

Questions and answers

Can a bot collect the necessary information up front?

No, SocialHub CX does not include a chatbot that questions customers up front; your team always writes and sends replies. The Co-Pilot helps internally by preparing follow-up questions and draft replies from your knowledge sources. For recurring follow-up questions, such as subscription number or line, your team uses templates with variables.

Why do transport companies refer people from social media to email, and how are the channels connected?

SocialHub CX handles social media and email in the same workspace, so a referral to email need not break the service. Requests involving personal data, such as subscriptions or refunds, often move from public comments to a private channel. In SocialHub CX the same team handles both tickets with the same templates and rules.

Through which channels do passengers reach a transport company's customer service?

SocialHub CX supports all social media networks SocialHub connects, WhatsApp, email and website chat for passenger communication. These include comments, direct messages and mentions on Facebook, Instagram, X and TikTok, for example, as well as reviews. All requests become tickets in one workspace, regardless of channel.
