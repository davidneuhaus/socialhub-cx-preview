    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    Does the SocialHub CX document pack cost anything?

    No, the SocialHub CX document pack is free and non-binding. You receive the service description, SocialHub's ISO 27001 certificate and further documents for data-protection, security and suitability reviews, tailored to your request, by email. A demo is not required, and there is no obligation to further talks.

    Can I use the SocialHub CX service specification in my tender?

    Yes, the SocialHub CX service specification is structured so you can use individual items for market research, specifications or evaluation matrices. Each item describes an existing function, and a separate section lists what is not included. You'll find it online and in the procurement & IT pack.

    What does the “We are planning a tender” checkbox mean for SocialHub CX?

    The checkbox helps SocialHub CX classify your request correctly. If you are planning a tender or price inquiry, we ensure communication that is clean under procurement law and send the documents you need for market research and suitability checks. The information is voluntary.

    Q&A

    Questions and answers

    Which contract clauses govern data transfers to third countries?

    For SocialHub CX, the data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR governs processing, including the sub-processors used. SocialHub CX data is hosted in Germany (Falkenstein, Nuremberg). SocialHub CX's AI runs on Microsoft Azure with an EU endpoint. Which additional clauses your data protection team requires can be clarified on the basis of the DPA.

    Does the BSI minimum standard also apply if the provider itself uses cloud sub-providers?

    SocialHub CX uses a hosting provider in Germany with a BSI C5 attestation; SocialHub itself does not hold its own C5 attestation. How the BSI minimum standard covers sub-providers is assessed by your authority. For this assessment, SocialHub provides its ISO 27001 certificate; further documents for data-protection, security and suitability reviews are tailored to your request.

    Is a social service tool a cloud service within the meaning of the BSI minimum standard?

    SocialHub CX is provided as software as a service. SocialHub CX is hosted in Germany (Falkenstein, Nuremberg) by a provider with a BSI C5 attestation; SocialHub itself does not hold its own C5 attestation. Whether that counts as an external cloud service under the BSI minimum standard is decided by your authority. SocialHub is ISO 27001 certified, and a data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR is available on request.

    Which providers must I list in the DORA register of information for a service tool?

    SocialHub CX is provided by maloon GmbH (SocialHub). SocialHub CX is hosted in Germany (Falkenstein, Nuremberg) by a provider with a BSI C5 attestation; SocialHub itself does not hold its own C5 attestation. SocialHub is ISO 27001 certified, and a data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR is available on request. Which providers you list in the DORA register of information and how you classify them is decided by your institution.

    Which accessibility test reports do contracting authorities require for software?

    SocialHub CX has been tested for accessibility according to BITV. Contracting authorities often require evidence of accessibility under BITV 2.0 or EN 301 549 for software. Further accessibility information is available on request. Which evidence is required in detail is set by the contracting authority in its tender documents.

    Is a web accessibility report sufficient for a software application?

    SocialHub CX has been tested for accessibility according to BITV. Further accessibility information is available on request. Whether a report for a website is also sufficient for a software application is decided by the contracting authority based on its requirements under BITV 2.0 and EN 301 549.
