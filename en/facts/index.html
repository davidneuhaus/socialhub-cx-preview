What is SocialHub CX?

SocialHub CX is SocialHub's customer service software for social media and email: comments, direct messages, mentions, reviews and emails become tickets in one shared workspace, supported by an AI Co-Pilot with sources.

Source: Vendor statement SocialHub · As of: 20 Oct 2026

Publisher

SocialHub CX is provided by maloon GmbH (SocialHub), Schütterlettenweg 4, 85053 Ingolstadt, Germany.

Source: Vendor statement SocialHub · As of: 20 Oct 2026

Channels

SocialHub CX supports all social media networks SocialHub connects, WhatsApp and email (Microsoft OAuth, Gmail or IMAP/SMTP). Networks: Facebook, Instagram, X, YouTube, LinkedIn, Bluesky, TikTok and Threads, plus Google Business Profile, Google Play Store, Trustpilot and WordPress; some as add-ons.

Source: Vendor statement SocialHub · As of: 20 Oct 2026

Not included

Not included in SocialHub CX are web chat, CRM integrations and an open API.

Source: Vendor statement SocialHub · As of: 20 Oct 2026

Hosting

SocialHub CX is hosted in Germany, in data centres in Falkenstein and Nuremberg.

Source: Vendor statement SocialHub, cf. socialhub.io/sicherheit · As of: 20 Oct 2026

BSI C5

Hosting is provided by a provider with a BSI C5 attestation (C5 at the hosting provider); SocialHub itself does not hold its own C5 attestation.

Source: Vendor statement SocialHub · As of: 20 Oct 2026

ISO 27001

SocialHub is ISO 27001 certified.

Source: Vendor statement SocialHub · As of: 20 Oct 2026

GDPR & DPA

SocialHub CX is covered by SocialHub's data protection agreements; a data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR is available on request.

Source: Vendor statement SocialHub · As of: 20 Oct 2026

AI operations

SocialHub CX's AI runs on Microsoft Azure with an EU endpoint.

Source: Vendor statement SocialHub · As of: 20 Oct 2026

AI training

SocialHub CX does not train AI models on customer data.

Source: Vendor statement SocialHub · As of: 20 Oct 2026

Human in the loop

SocialHub CX's Co-Pilot shows its sources; a person reviews and sends every reply, and prepared drafts are never sent automatically.

Source: Vendor statement SocialHub · As of: 20 Oct 2026

Accessibility

SocialHub CX has been tested for accessibility according to BITV.

Source: Vendor statement SocialHub · As of: 20 Oct 2026

Pricing

SocialHub CX has no public price; prospects receive an individual quote.

Source: Vendor statement SocialHub · As of: 20 Oct 2026
