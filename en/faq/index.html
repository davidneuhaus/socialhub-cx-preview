FAQ

Frequently asked questions about SocialHub CX

Here we answer the most frequent questions about SocialHub CX – on features, AI, security, data protection, pricing and procurement.

FAQ

Product & channels

What is SocialHub CX?

SocialHub CX is customer service software for social media, WhatsApp, email and website chat. Requests from comments, direct messages, mentions, reviews and mailboxes become tickets in one shared workspace. It adds templates, routing, dashboards and an AI Co-Pilot with sources. The provider is SocialHub, a product of maloon GmbH in Ingolstadt, Germany.

Who is SocialHub CX for?

SocialHub CX is for service and social media teams that answer many written requests and care about data protection. Typical users are transport companies, retailers and brands, loyalty programmes, banks and insurers, municipalities and utilities – especially mid-sized and large organisations and public buyers in the DACH region.

Which channels does SocialHub CX support?

SocialHub CX supports email, WhatsApp, website chat and social media. You connect mailboxes via Microsoft (OAuth), Gmail or IMAP/SMTP. The Social module offers Facebook, Instagram, X, YouTube, LinkedIn, Bluesky, TikTok and Threads, plus Google Business Profile, Google Play Store, Trustpilot and WordPress, some of them as add-ons.

Where is SocialHub CX data hosted?

SocialHub CX is hosted in Germany (Falkenstein, Nuremberg), like the entire SocialHub platform. Hosting is provided by a provider with a BSI C5 attestation; SocialHub itself does not hold its own C5 attestation. SocialHub is ISO 27001 certified, and a GDPR data processing agreement is available on request.

How does SocialHub CX differ from Sprinklr or Zendesk?

SocialHub CX focuses on social media, WhatsApp, email and website chat and comes from a German provider hosting in Germany. Sprinklr is a unified CXM platform for large enterprises, Zendesk a broad service system with many channels and add-ons. Which fits depends on your channels, team size and compliance requirements.

How can I get to know SocialHub CX?

The best way to get to know SocialHub CX is a demo. We show you the workspace with examples from your industry and address your channels and requirements. For a start in live operation we offer a pilot; for procurement and IT we provide documents.

Can I connect Microsoft 365 mailboxes to SocialHub CX?

Yes, SocialHub CX connects Microsoft 365 mailboxes via Microsoft OAuth, Gmail mailboxes via Gmail; other mailboxes connect via IMAP/SMTP. Each mailbox has its own settings for routing, signature and automatic acknowledgement. With “Sync now” you fetch new messages immediately when needed.

How does SocialHub CX distribute new tickets?

SocialHub CX distributes new email tickets per mailbox using keyword rules first. If no rule matches, they go to a team, a person, round robin, or stay deliberately unassigned. Staff set to “Away” receive no new tickets; manual assignment is possible at any time.

Can SocialHub CX measure service levels?

Yes, SocialHub CX measures a first-response target, counted in business hours and selectable from one to 24 hours or custom. The dashboard shows on-time and late first responses per team and person. Other SLA types such as resolution targets or escalation rules are not part of the product.

Which metrics does SocialHub CX show?

SocialHub CX's email dashboard shows total, solved and unsolved tickets, median first-response time, late first responses, resolution time, one-touch rate and reopens, among others. The social dashboard shows response rate, response and handling time and the sentiment of incoming messages by channel and type.

FAQ

AI Co-Pilot

Does SocialHub CX's AI reply to customers on its own?

No, SocialHub CX does not send AI replies on its own. The Co-Pilot creates drafts and summaries, but a person reviews and sends every reply. Even automatically prepared suggestions are never sent by themselves. The Co-Pilot shows the sources of its drafts from your knowledge base and past conversations so your team can check statements quickly.

Where does SocialHub CX's AI run?

SocialHub CX's AI runs on Microsoft Azure with an EU endpoint. The Co-Pilot drafts replies and summaries based on your knowledge base and past conversations and shows its sources. A person sends every reply. SocialHub CX data itself is hosted in Germany (Falkenstein, Nuremberg).

Where does SocialHub CX's Co-Pilot get its knowledge?

SocialHub CX's Co-Pilot uses the ticket subject and latest messages, matching help-centre articles and files, up to three similar solved tickets from past conversations and the chat history so far. For the knowledge base, you connect your own or use SocialHub's. It shows the sources of its answer. If an article and a learned ticket disagree, the article wins.

Does SocialHub CX use solved tickets for the AI?

SocialHub CX only uses solved tickets if you switch on “Learn from solved tickets”; it is off by default. Then tickets from the last twelve months serve as anonymised summaries. Content your team repeatedly rates as wrong lands in a review queue and is removed.

Does SocialHub CX use our customer data to train the AI?

SocialHub CX does not train AI models on customer data. The Co-Pilot only uses solved tickets if you activate “Learn from solved tickets”; it is off by default. Once activated, tickets from the last twelve months serve as anonymised summaries for suggestions in your account. Content your team repeatedly rates as wrong is removed.

FAQ

Security & privacy

Where is SocialHub CX hosted?

SocialHub CX is hosted in Germany (Falkenstein, Nuremberg). Data is processed in our subcontractor's data centre in compliance with GDPR and BDSG on the basis of data processing agreements. The hosting provider holds a BSI C5 attestation; data transfer is secured via SSL/SSH, VPN tunnels and firewalls.

Does SocialHub CX have its own BSI C5 attestation?

No, SocialHub CX and SocialHub do not hold their own BSI C5 attestation. Hosting is provided by a provider with a BSI C5 attestation. According to the BSI, a C5 attestation always applies to specific audited cloud services, not to a company as a whole. Further hosting information is available on request.

Is SocialHub CX ISO 27001 certified?

SocialHub CX is part of SocialHub, and SocialHub is ISO 27001 certified. The certification covers SocialHub's information security management system, not the hosting provider. In addition, SocialHub undergoes regular penetration tests. The certificate is included in the procurement & IT documents.

Is there a GDPR data processing agreement for SocialHub CX?

Yes, for SocialHub CX a data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR is available on request. The same data protection agreements apply as for the SocialHub platform. As a German company, SocialHub complies with the BDSG and the EU GDPR.

Does SocialHub CX support single sign-on?

Yes, SocialHub CX supports single sign-on (SSO). Your team signs in through your organisation's central login, and accounts are managed via the SocialHub platform. We clarify your IT's requirements for the connection in a conversation.

Is SocialHub CX accessible under BITV 2.0?

SocialHub CX has been tested for accessibility according to BITV. For public buyers who require accessibility under BITV 2.0 and EN 301 549 in their specifications, this is important evidence. Further accessibility information is available on request.

Is SocialHub CX suitable for public tenders?

SocialHub CX is suitable for tenders on social media, customer dialogue and email management, not for tenders whose focus is on other channels. For suitability and security reviews you receive the service description (available online), SocialHub's ISO 27001 certificate and further documents for data-protection, security and suitability reviews, tailored to your request.

FAQ

Pricing, demo & procurement

Does the SocialHub CX document pack cost anything?

No, the SocialHub CX document pack is free and non-binding. You receive the service description, SocialHub's ISO 27001 certificate and further documents for data-protection, security and suitability reviews, tailored to your request, by email. A demo is not required, and there is no obligation to further talks.

Can I use the SocialHub CX service specification in my tender?

Yes, the SocialHub CX service specification is structured so you can use individual items for market research, specifications or evaluation matrices. Each item describes an existing function, and a separate section lists what is not included. You'll find it online and in the procurement & IT pack.

What does the “We are planning a tender” checkbox mean for SocialHub CX?

The checkbox helps SocialHub CX classify your request correctly. If you are planning a tender or price inquiry, we ensure communication that is clean under procurement law and send the documents you need for market research and suitability checks. The information is voluntary.

What is the difference between a demo and a pilot of SocialHub CX?

With SocialHub CX, the demo is a guided presentation and the pilot is use in your own day-to-day work. In the demo you see the features using examples. In the pilot your team works with real mailboxes and social media profiles. We agree scope, duration and terms of the pilot individually.

Is there a free trial of SocialHub CX?

No, we do not offer a freely accessible trial of SocialHub CX. Instead, we show you SocialHub CX in a demo and offer a pilot in which your team works with its own channels. That way you get a setup that fits your mailboxes and profiles.

May I use the SocialHub CX service specification in a tender?

Yes, you may use items from the SocialHub CX service specification for market research, specifications or evaluation matrices. In the procurement procedure, make sure to use product-neutral wording. Numbers stay stable, so you can refer to individual items unambiguously in bidder questions.

Is the SocialHub CX service specification available as a PDF?

Yes, the SocialHub CX service specification is print-friendly and available online; you receive it as a PDF with the procurement & IT documents. The online version is always current and dated. When changes occur, the numbers of existing items are kept.

Does SocialHub CX support migration and onboarding?

Yes, migration and onboarding are possible with SocialHub CX. SocialHub supports your team when switching from an existing system and when introducing it into day-to-day work. Which data is transferred and how onboarding runs is clarified on request, tailored to your starting point.

What does SocialHub CX cost?

SocialHub CX has no public price; you receive an individual quote. The price depends on channels, seats and the scope you need. A start in live operation is also possible as a pilot. You'll receive the quote shortly after your request.
