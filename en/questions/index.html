Social media and email

Can I handle Facebook and Instagram comments, not just direct messages, as tickets in the service inbox?

Yes, SocialHub CX turns Facebook and Instagram comments into tickets, not just direct messages. In the Social module, comments, direct messages, mentions, posts and reviews from connected profiles land as tickets and can be filtered by message type, channel and date range. Your team handles public and private requests in the same workspace as emails.

How do I categorise and analyse complaints from social media?

SocialHub CX categorises complaints via tags your team sets or AI assigns automatically on request, and analyses them in dashboards. Rule-based folders group topics continuously. The social dashboard shows tickets, response rate, response time and the breakdown by type, channel and sentiment. In the email dashboard you compare periods with the previous period or year.

Do Instagram direct messages arrive in the service inbox?

Yes, SocialHub CX turns Instagram direct messages into tickets in the Social module, as well as comments, mentions and posts. Your team filters by channel and message type and handles Instagram requests in the same workspace as emails, WhatsApp, website chats and other networks. The Instagram profile needs to be connected as a channel.

Can I handle channels such as email, Instagram and Facebook in separate views?

Yes, SocialHub CX separates email and social media into two modules and offers filters and views in both. In the Social module you filter by channel, such as Instagram or Facebook, and by message type; in the Email module by mailbox, tag, status and assignee. Rule-based folders save frequent views permanently for the whole team.

Does the tool bring all channels together in one place, or do I have to switch tools?

SocialHub CX brings email, WhatsApp, website chat and social media together in one workspace, so your team doesn't have to switch tools. You handle emails in the Email module and social media messages in the Social module, both with the same ticket logic of status, priority, owner and tags.

How easy is it to connect an existing email mailbox?

SocialHub CX connects existing mailboxes via Microsoft (OAuth) or IMAP/SMTP, without you changing your email address. Per mailbox you set routing, signature and automatic acknowledgement. SocialHub CX fetches new messages continuously; with “Sync now” it happens immediately at the push of a button.

Is there a Gmail integration?

Yes, SocialHub CX connects Gmail mailboxes, as well as mailboxes via Microsoft (OAuth) or IMAP/SMTP. Every incoming email becomes a ticket with a ticket number, and per mailbox you set routing, signature and automatic acknowledgement. How many mailboxes your package includes is clarified on request.

How do signatures per mailbox work?

SocialHub CX stores signatures per mailbox and applies them automatically when replying. This way, replies from the “Subscriptions” mailbox carry a different signature than replies from “Complaints”, without staff having to remember. In addition, an automatic acknowledgement with ticket number can be set up per mailbox.

Who takes over tickets when someone on the team is away?

In SocialHub CX, staff with the status “Away” receive no new tickets; distribution runs by rule to a team, a person or round robin among available colleagues. Tickets already assigned can be reassigned manually at any time. The “Unassigned” view shows what has no owner yet.

Can a bot collect the necessary information up front?

No, SocialHub CX does not include a chatbot that questions customers up front; your team always writes and sends replies. The Co-Pilot helps internally by preparing follow-up questions and draft replies from your knowledge sources. For recurring follow-up questions, such as subscription number or line, your team uses templates with variables.

Does the tool support WhatsApp Business?

Yes, SocialHub CX offers WhatsApp as a channel; which packages include WhatsApp is clarified on request. Your team handles WhatsApp messages in the same workspace as social media messages and emails. Single sign-on (SSO) is also available for SocialHub CX, with packaging likewise on request.

Who is SocialHub CX suitable for?

SocialHub CX suits service teams that handle many requests via social media, WhatsApp, email and website chat, for example in public transport, retail and loyalty, banking and insurance, public administration and municipal utilities. Typical users are teams that manage several mailboxes and profiles together, want clear ownership and measure first responses.

How do we handle requests from Instagram DMs and X replies when email runs elsewhere?

SocialHub CX brings Instagram direct messages, replies on X and emails together in one workspace, so no separate tool is needed. Social media messages land as tickets in the Social module, emails in the Email module. Both use the same logic of status, priority, owner and tags, and your team works with the same templates and the same Co-Pilot.

What is the difference between a social media management tool and a helpdesk with social channels?

SocialHub CX is customer service software for social media, WhatsApp, email and website chat: it turns social media messages and emails into tickets, with service metrics in the social and email dashboards. A social media management tool is built mainly for planning, publishing and reporting on content. Service needs routing, templates, internal notes, an activity log and service metrics, and that is exactly what SocialHub CX is designed for.

Which tools handle not just direct messages but also comments and mentions?

SocialHub CX handles comments, mentions, posts and reviews as tickets in addition to direct messages. This applies to the networks SocialHub connects, including Facebook, Instagram, X, YouTube, LinkedIn, TikTok, Google Business and Trustpilot; some of them as add-ons. Your team filters by message type and channel and handles everything in the same workspace as emails.

How do teams handle a very high number of comments per day in one shared inbox?

SocialHub CX helps with high comment volumes through rule-based folders, filters, templates and bulk actions. Folders group topics continuously, templates inserted with “/” keep replies consistent, and bulk actions mark several tickets done, set priority or assign them to yourself at once. On request, AI assigns tags. The dashboard shows arrivals, backlog and response time so you can plan shifts.

When is a social media tool too much of a marketing tool for customer service?

SocialHub CX is built for the point where social media becomes a service channel: then your team needs tickets with ownership rather than just an inbox. Typical signs are missing assignment, no first-response target, no internal notes and no email in the same workflow. If these are missing, a marketing tool is usually too limited for service.

How do we manage requests from several social media profiles in one view?

SocialHub CX shows requests from all connected social media profiles in one shared ticket list in the Social module. Filters by channel, message type and date range narrow the view, and rule-based folders save frequent views for the whole team. This way your team keeps track even with many profiles, without switching accounts.

How do we bring order to messy customer tickets?

SocialHub CX organises tickets by status, priority, owner and tags as well as rule-based folders that keep updating. Views such as Priority, My tickets, Unassigned and Snoozed show what is pending. Keyword rules per mailbox distribute new emails automatically, and the activity log records who changed what and when.

How do costs develop when colleagues help out occasionally?

SocialHub CX has no public price; how colleagues who only help occasionally are reflected in the quote is clarified on request. Packages for SocialHub CX, for example for WhatsApp, single sign-on or the number of mailboxes, are likewise agreed on request. This way the quote fits your actual team size.

How long does a SocialHub CX demo take?

We tailor SocialHub CX demos to your request and your questions; the length is available on request. We show, for example, email ticketing, the Social module, the Co-Pilot and dashboards using your typical requests. Colleagues from service, IT or procurement are welcome to join.

AI co-pilot

Where does the AI get its answers, and how do I prevent made-up content?

SocialHub CX lets the Co-Pilot answer only from sources you approve and shows those sources with every answer. These include your knowledge base, your own or SocialHub's, and past conversations in the form of solved tickets. If an article and a learned ticket disagree, the article wins. Your team checks every draft before sending, and content rated as wrong lands in a review queue.

How do I make sure sensitive replies, e.g. on refunds, data protection or legal matters, are approved by a person?

In SocialHub CX a person always approves every reply, because the Co-Pilot sends nothing itself. It creates drafts and summaries; even automatically prepared suggestions are never sent automatically. A multi-step approval workflow with a second person is not part of the product. For sensitive topics, aligned templates, internal notes and assignment to experienced colleagues help.

Does the AI run in the EU?

SocialHub CX's AI runs on Microsoft Azure with an EU endpoint. The Co-Pilot drafts replies and summaries based on your knowledge sources and shows them; a person sends every reply. SocialHub CX does not train AI models on customer data. All other SocialHub CX data is hosted in Germany (Falkenstein, Nuremberg).

Does the AI pull content from unsuitable articles, and how do I correct that?

SocialHub CX shows the sources used with every Co-Pilot answer, so your team spots unsuitable articles immediately. Your team rates answers; learned content repeatedly rated as wrong lands in a review queue and is removed. You correct the article itself in the knowledge base, because articles take precedence over learned tickets.

Does the AI learn from old tickets, including bad replies?

SocialHub CX only learns from solved tickets if you switch on the option “Learn from solved tickets”; it is off by default. Then tickets from the last twelve months serve as anonymised summaries. The feedback loop catches bad content: entries repeatedly rated as wrong are reviewed and removed. SocialHub CX does not train AI models on customer data.

How do I measure whether AI suggestions are used and helpful?

SocialHub CX shows Co-Pilot metrics in the dashboard: replies with Co-Pilot, accepted drafts and answers rated helpful. In addition, your team rates individual answers; ratings are available in a feedback overview with CSV export. Content repeatedly rated as wrong lands in a review queue.

How do agents rate AI answers in the ticket, and how is that fed back?

In SocialHub CX agents rate Co-Pilot answers directly in the ticket, and these ratings steer knowledge quality. Content repeatedly rated as wrong lands in a review queue and is removed. Ratings can be exported as CSV. SocialHub CX does not train AI models on customer data.

Does SocialHub CX use AI, and for what?

Yes, SocialHub CX uses AI in the Co-Pilot, which creates draft replies, summaries and answers to internal questions from your approved knowledge sources and shows the sources. On request, AI assigns tags automatically. A person sends every reply. SocialHub CX's AI runs on Microsoft Azure with an EU endpoint. SocialHub CX does not train AI models on customer data.

Can AI draft replies from our documents without sending them itself?

Yes, SocialHub CX's Co-Pilot drafts replies from your approved help-centre articles and files as well as past conversations and never sends on its own. It shows the sources with every draft, and your team reviews, edits and sends. Even automatically prepared suggestions are never sent automatically. SocialHub CX's AI runs on Microsoft Azure with an EU endpoint.

How do we keep AI answers in support accurate?

SocialHub CX keeps Co-Pilot answers accurate by using only approved sources, showing them and letting a person send every reply. Articles take precedence over learned tickets. Your team rates answers; content repeatedly rated as wrong lands in a review queue and is removed. Up-to-date help-centre articles are therefore the most important lever.

Does the AI recognise when it must hand over to a person?

With SocialHub CX the AI never needs to hand over, because the Co-Pilot never writes to customers itself: a person reviews and sends every reply. The Co-Pilot creates drafts and summaries with sources. A chatbot that talks to customers on its own is not part of the product.

Can AI help with the same recurring questions from our documents?

Yes, SocialHub CX helps with recurring questions through a Co-Pilot that drafts replies from your help-centre articles, files and past conversations and shows its sources. In addition, your team inserts aligned templates with variables using “/”. Optionally, the Co-Pilot learns from solved tickets of the last twelve months as anonymised summaries; your team always sends.

May incoming customer emails be analysed by a language model to create draft replies?

With SocialHub CX this is possible under clear conditions: the AI only drafts, and a person reviews and sends every reply. SocialHub CX's AI runs on Microsoft Azure with an EU endpoint. SocialHub CX does not train AI models on customer data. A data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR is available on request. Whether this use is permissible in your organisation is assessed by your data protection team based on this information.

How much automation makes sense in support, and where does the person stay involved?

SocialHub CX automates distribution and preparation, while a person decides on and sends every reply. Keyword rules and round robin distribute tickets, AI assigns tags on request, and the Co-Pilot prepares drafts and summaries. There are no automatically sent replies, so all external communication stays in your team's hands.

Service levels and analytics

Why does the SLA timer show a different time than expected?

SocialHub CX counts the first-response target in business hours, not calendar hours, so the time shown can differ from the clock. In addition, a newly saved target only applies to tickets that arrive afterwards. You set the target to 1, 2, 4, 8 or 24 business hours or a custom value.

How long do bank customers wait for written replies?

SocialHub CX shows banks and insurers their own waiting time for written replies instead of relying on industry figures. You set a first-response target in business hours, and the dashboard shows median first-response time, late first responses and resolution time per team. Keyword rules route emails directly to departments such as accounts, cards or claims.

How quickly do direct banks reply by email?

SocialHub CX measures how quickly your institution replies by email: the dashboard shows median first-response time, late first responses, resolution time and one-touch rate, compared with the previous period. A first-response target in business hours makes delays visible. We don't quote industry figures; your own value is what counts.

Which metrics should a social team measure at high comment volumes?

SocialHub CX provides the key metrics for social teams in the social dashboard: tickets, handled tickets, response rate, response time, handling time and the breakdown by type, channel and sentiment. In the social dashboard you choose the date range; comparisons by team and person and with the previous period are offered by the email dashboard.
