Use cases: Utilities

How service teams in utilities handle typical requests with SocialHub CX.

Instalments, meter readings and moves: routing emails automatically

The service mailbox receives many emails every day on instalment changes, meter readings and moves. So far one person sorts everything by hand each morning.

Emails reach the right team without manual sorting, customers receive an acknowledgement immediately, and replies are consistent.

How it works in SocialHub CX

  1. The mailbox is connected via Microsoft (OAuth), Gmail or IMAP/SMTP.
  2. Keyword rules (“instalment”, “meter reading”, “move”) assign tickets to the responsible team, then round robin.
  3. An automatic acknowledgement with ticket number goes to the sender.
  4. Templates and Co-Pilot drafts from your service articles speed up replies.
  5. Held emails such as out-of-office replies appear separately with a reason.

Supply outage on social media and by email

Power fails in one district. Comments, messages and emails ask about cause and duration – while the grid control centre is at work.

All channels receive the same information, and the team sees when request volume drops again.

How it works in SocialHub CX

  1. All requests are captured as tickets in the workspace.
  2. A rule-based “Outage” folder bundles the tickets.
  3. Communications uses an aligned template with the current status and updates it centrally when new information arrives.
  4. Bulk actions close similar tickets once everyone is informed.
  5. The social dashboard shows volume and sentiment during the outage.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can SocialHub CX assign meter-reading emails automatically?

Yes, SocialHub CX assigns emails with terms such as “meter reading” to a team automatically via keyword rules. Transferring the values to your billing system is not part of the product, because SocialHub CX includes no system connection; your team transfers them as usual.

Does SocialHub CX send an acknowledgement?

Yes, on request SocialHub CX sends an automatic acknowledgement with ticket number to the sender, per mailbox. You define the text per mailbox. Out-of-office replies and bounces are held back and do not trigger tickets in the normal flow.

How does SocialHub CX help during a power outage?

SocialHub CX helps during a power outage by landing all requests from social media, WhatsApp and email as tickets in an outage folder. Your team replies with a centrally maintained template and marks similar tickets as done in bulk once the outage is resolved.

Can SocialHub CX publish outage information automatically?

No, SocialHub CX does not publish posts or send automatic replies. SocialHub CX is designed for handling incoming requests. For posts you use your usual tools; your team sends replies to requests with aligned templates.
