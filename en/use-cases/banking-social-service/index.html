Moving social media requests into service in an orderly way

Moving social media requests into service in an orderly way

SocialHub CX turns comments, direct messages and mentions into tickets with an owner and activity log, so social media requests are handled traceably in customer service.

Request a demo

Starting point

Customers ask service questions under marketing posts or by direct message. So far, the social media team handles them on the side, without a clear handover to service.

How it works in SocialHub CX

  1. Comments, direct messages and mentions become tickets in the Social module.
  2. Service tickets are assigned to the service team; the social media team keeps an overview of all messages.
  3. Internal notes document questions to departments.
  4. Replies are written with templates; a person sends every reply.
  5. Activity log and customer history make the course traceable.

Outcome

Service requests from social media have a clear owner and a documented history; marketing and service work in the same workspace.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does SocialHub CX separate service requests from marketing comments?

SocialHub CX separates service requests via filters, tags, rule-based folders and assignment to teams. The service team handles its tickets, the social media team keeps all messages in view. AI can tag new tickets on request so topics are sorted faster.

Does SocialHub CX's AI send replies to bank customers?

No, SocialHub CX's AI does not send replies. The Co-Pilot creates drafts and summaries with sources; a person reviews and sends every reply. Even automatically prepared suggestions are never sent by themselves. SocialHub CX's AI runs on Microsoft Azure with an EU endpoint.

Is handling documented in SocialHub CX?

Yes, SocialHub CX documents every ticket change in the activity log: status, owner, priority, tags and replies with time and person. Internal notes stay on the ticket. The customer history shows all tickets of a person over time.
