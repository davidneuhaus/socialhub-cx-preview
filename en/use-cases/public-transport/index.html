Use cases: Public transport

How service teams in public transport handle typical requests with SocialHub CX.

Handling disruption reports on social media and by email

A line goes down. Within a short time, comments under posts, direct messages, WhatsApp messages and emails ask about replacement services, connections and refunds – on several channels at once.

Your team sees all disruption requests in one place, replies consistently and can see in the dashboard whether the first-response target was met.

How it works in SocialHub CX

  1. All messages land as tickets in the workspace – emails in the Email module, comments, direct messages and mentions in the Social module.
  2. A rule-based folder, e.g. “Disruption”, collects matching tickets; AI tagging adds tags on request.
  3. Keyword rules per mailbox route “refund” questions straight to the responsible team.
  4. Your team replies with an aligned disruption template via “/”, personalised with variables such as first name.
  5. Similar tickets are marked done in bulk; the dashboard shows volume, backlog and on-time first responses.

Answering questions on subscriptions, Deutschlandticket and refunds

The subscription centre receives many similar questions every day: cancellation, change of address, Deutschlandticket, refunds after delays. They arrive by email, but also as direct messages or WhatsApp messages.

Recurring questions are answered consistently and traceably; new colleagues find answers faster via templates and the Co-Pilot.

How it works in SocialHub CX

  1. Emails are assigned to the subscription team by keyword rule (“cancellation”, “Deutschlandticket”, “refund”), round robin to available staff.
  2. Direct messages and WhatsApp messages land as tickets in the Social module and are assigned to the team.
  3. Your team inserts templates with variables and links matching help-centre articles.
  4. The Co-Pilot drafts replies based on your fare conditions and shows the sources.
  5. Optionally, the Co-Pilot learns from solved tickets of the last twelve months – as anonymised summaries.

Passing praise and complaints to departments

One passenger praises a driver, another complains about broken air conditioning. Customer service replies but needs feedback from operations or the workshop.

Departments are involved without information getting lost in email forwards; the history stays traceable on the ticket.

How it works in SocialHub CX

  1. The ticket is assigned to the responsible team or stays with service, with colleagues from the department as watchers.
  2. Questions go through internal notes that passengers never see.
  3. Watchers are notified of status and owner changes.
  4. Tags such as “praise” or “vehicle” make topics analysable in the dashboard later.
  5. The activity log shows who did what and when.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can SocialHub CX collect disruption requests automatically?

Yes, SocialHub CX collects disruption requests in rule-based folders that keep updating, for example by tags or channel. In addition, AI can tag new tickets. Your team sees all requests about a disruption in one view and works through them with templates and bulk actions.

Can SocialHub CX reply automatically during disruptions?

No, SocialHub CX does not send replies automatically; a person sends every reply. Aligned templates via “/” and Co-Pilot drafts speed up replying. For emails, each mailbox has an automatic acknowledgement with ticket number, which you can word for disruption situations.

Can SocialHub CX's Co-Pilot use our fare conditions?

Yes, SocialHub CX's Co-Pilot uses help-centre articles and files you add as knowledge sources, for example fare conditions or refund rules. It shows the sources with every answer. If articles and learned tickets disagree, the article wins; your team always sends.

How does SocialHub CX distribute subscription requests?

SocialHub CX distributes subscription requests by email via keyword rules per mailbox, for example for “cancellation” or “Deutschlandticket”. Tickets then go to the subscription team, a person or round robin. Anyone away receives no new tickets; social media tickets you assign manually.

Can departments work in SocialHub CX?

Yes, in SocialHub CX departments work on tickets as their own teams or as watchers. Questions and answers go through internal notes that customers never see. Watchers are notified of status and owner changes, in-product and via browser push.

Can I analyse complaints by topic in SocialHub CX?

Yes, SocialHub CX analyses complaints via tags your team sets or AI assigns automatically on request. In the dashboard you filter by team and person; the social dashboard shows the breakdown by channel, type and sentiment. You compare the period with the previous period or year.
