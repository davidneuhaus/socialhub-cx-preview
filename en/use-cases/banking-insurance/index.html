Use cases: Banking & insurance

Use cases: Banking & insurance

How service teams in banking & insurance handle typical requests with SocialHub CX.

Request a demo

SocialHub CX covers two typical workflows at banks and insurers: moving social media requests into service in an orderly way and routing service mailboxes by department. Each workflow shows how ownership and history stay traceable per ticket.

Moving social media requests into service in an orderly way

Customers ask service questions under marketing posts or by direct message. So far, the social media team handles them on the side, without a clear handover to service.

Service requests from social media have a clear owner and a documented history; marketing and service work in the same workspace.

How it works in SocialHub CX

  1. Comments, direct messages and mentions become tickets in the Social module.
  2. Service tickets are assigned to the service team; the social media team keeps an overview of all messages.
  3. Internal notes document questions to departments.
  4. Replies are written with templates; a person sends every reply.
  5. Activity log and customer history make the course traceable.

Routing service mailboxes by department

Several shared mailboxes – for account, card and claims, for instance – are handled by different teams. Emails often land in the wrong mailbox and are forwarded manually.

Emails reach the right department without manual forwarding, and management sees whether first responses are on target.

How it works in SocialHub CX

  1. Each mailbox is connected via Microsoft (OAuth), Gmail or IMAP/SMTP, with its own signature and acknowledgement.
  2. Keyword rules per mailbox assign emails to the right team, then person or round robin.
  3. Staff set to “Away” receive no new tickets.
  4. A first-response target in business hours applies to new tickets.
  5. The dashboard shows on-time and late first responses per team and person.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does SocialHub CX separate service requests from marketing comments?

SocialHub CX separates service requests via filters, tags, rule-based folders and assignment to teams. The service team handles its tickets, the social media team keeps all messages in view. AI can tag new tickets on request so topics are sorted faster.

Does SocialHub CX's AI send replies to bank customers?

No, SocialHub CX's AI does not send replies. The Co-Pilot creates drafts and summaries with sources; a person reviews and sends every reply. Even automatically prepared suggestions are never sent by themselves. SocialHub CX's AI runs on Microsoft Azure with an EU endpoint.

How many mailboxes can I connect to SocialHub CX?

SocialHub CX connects several mailboxes, each via Microsoft OAuth, Gmail or IMAP/SMTP, with its own settings for routing, signature and acknowledgement. This way one team handles the mailboxes for accounts, cards and claims together. How many mailboxes your package includes is clarified on request.

Can SocialHub CX assign emails to a department by content?

Yes, SocialHub CX assigns emails to a team or person via keyword rules per mailbox before the default assignment applies. If no rule matches, the ticket goes to a team, a person, round robin, or stays deliberately unassigned.
