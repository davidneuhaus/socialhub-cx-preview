Handling disruption reports on social media and by email

Handling disruption reports on social media and by email

SocialHub CX bundles disruption requests from social media, WhatsApp and email as tickets, sorts them into a disruption folder and helps with consistent replies through templates.

Request a demo

Starting point

A line goes down. Within a short time, comments under posts, direct messages, WhatsApp messages and emails ask about replacement services, connections and refunds – on several channels at once.

How it works in SocialHub CX

  1. All messages land as tickets in the workspace – emails in the Email module, comments, direct messages and mentions in the Social module.
  2. A rule-based folder, e.g. “Disruption”, collects matching tickets; AI tagging adds tags on request.
  3. Keyword rules per mailbox route “refund” questions straight to the responsible team.
  4. Your team replies with an aligned disruption template via “/”, personalised with variables such as first name.
  5. Similar tickets are marked done in bulk; the dashboard shows volume, backlog and on-time first responses.

Outcome

Your team sees all disruption requests in one place, replies consistently and can see in the dashboard whether the first-response target was met.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can SocialHub CX collect disruption requests automatically?

Yes, SocialHub CX collects disruption requests in rule-based folders that keep updating, for example by tags or channel. In addition, AI can tag new tickets. Your team sees all requests about a disruption in one view and works through them with templates and bulk actions.

Can SocialHub CX reply automatically during disruptions?

No, SocialHub CX does not send replies automatically; a person sends every reply. Aligned templates via “/” and Co-Pilot drafts speed up replying. For emails, each mailbox has an automatic acknowledgement with ticket number, which you can word for disruption situations.

Does our team see in SocialHub CX how disruptions affect service?

Yes, SocialHub CX shows volume and backlog over time, arrival times by hour and weekday, and on-time and late first responses in the dashboard. The social dashboard shows the breakdown by channel, type and sentiment. This lets you compare disruption days with normal days.
