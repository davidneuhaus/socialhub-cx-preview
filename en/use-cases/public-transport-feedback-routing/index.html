Passing praise and complaints to departments

Passing praise and complaints to departments

SocialHub CX helps pass praise and complaints from social media and email to departments such as operations or drivers – with teams, internal notes, watchers and an activity log.

Request a demo

Starting point

One passenger praises a driver, another complains about broken air conditioning. Customer service replies but needs feedback from operations or the workshop.

How it works in SocialHub CX

  1. The ticket is assigned to the responsible team or stays with service, with colleagues from the department as watchers.
  2. Questions go through internal notes that passengers never see.
  3. Watchers are notified of status and owner changes.
  4. Tags such as “praise” or “vehicle” make topics analysable in the dashboard later.
  5. The activity log shows who did what and when.

Outcome

Departments are involved without information getting lost in email forwards; the history stays traceable on the ticket.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can departments work in SocialHub CX?

Yes, in SocialHub CX departments work on tickets as their own teams or as watchers. Questions and answers go through internal notes that customers never see. Watchers are notified of status and owner changes, in-product and via browser push.

Can I analyse complaints by topic in SocialHub CX?

Yes, SocialHub CX analyses complaints via tags your team sets or AI assigns automatically on request. In the dashboard you filter by team and person; the social dashboard shows the breakdown by channel, type and sentiment. You compare the period with the previous period or year.

Can we trace in SocialHub CX who handled a complaint?

Yes, SocialHub CX keeps an activity log for every ticket with all changes to status, owner, priority and tags. Internal notes stay on the ticket. Even weeks later you can trace who handled and answered a complaint, and when.
