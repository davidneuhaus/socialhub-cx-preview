Routing service mailboxes by department

Routing service mailboxes by department

SocialHub CX routes emails from several service mailboxes to departments such as account, card or claims via keyword rules and measures the first-response target.

Request a demo

Starting point

Several shared mailboxes – for account, card and claims, for instance – are handled by different teams. Emails often land in the wrong mailbox and are forwarded manually.

How it works in SocialHub CX

  1. Each mailbox is connected via Microsoft (OAuth), Gmail or IMAP/SMTP, with its own signature and acknowledgement.
  2. Keyword rules per mailbox assign emails to the right team, then person or round robin.
  3. Staff set to “Away” receive no new tickets.
  4. A first-response target in business hours applies to new tickets.
  5. The dashboard shows on-time and late first responses per team and person.

Outcome

Emails reach the right department without manual forwarding, and management sees whether first responses are on target.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How many mailboxes can I connect to SocialHub CX?

SocialHub CX connects several mailboxes, each via Microsoft OAuth, Gmail or IMAP/SMTP, with its own settings for routing, signature and acknowledgement. This way one team handles the mailboxes for accounts, cards and claims together. How many mailboxes your package includes is clarified on request.

Can SocialHub CX assign emails to a department by content?

Yes, SocialHub CX assigns emails to a team or person via keyword rules per mailbox before the default assignment applies. If no rule matches, the ticket goes to a team, a person, round robin, or stays deliberately unassigned.

Can SocialHub CX analyse service levels per department?

SocialHub CX analyses the first-response target per team and person: the dashboard shows on-time and late first responses and can be filtered by team. The target itself applies account-wide, selectable from one to 24 business hours or custom.
