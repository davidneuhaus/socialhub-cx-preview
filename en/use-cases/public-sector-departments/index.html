Clarifying responsibilities across departments

Clarifying responsibilities across departments

SocialHub CX maps departments as teams, so requests are assigned, coordinated internally and answered traceably – without email forwarding chains.

Request a demo

Starting point

A question about a road closure concerns both the public order office and the civil engineering office. So far the email gets forwarded, and nobody knows who replies.

How it works in SocialHub CX

  1. Departments are created as teams; tickets are assigned to a team or person.
  2. Other departments follow the ticket as watchers.
  3. Coordination happens in internal notes on the ticket.
  4. If the reply depends on feedback, the ticket is set to “Waiting” or snoozed until a set time.
  5. The activity log shows every step.

Outcome

Every request has a clear owner, coordination stays on the ticket, and the handling path is traceable.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can SocialHub CX map several departments?

Yes, SocialHub CX maps departments as teams that have their own views and serve as assignment targets. Keyword rules per mailbox can assign emails directly to a department. Other participants follow tickets as watchers and are notified of changes.

Do citizens see internal coordination in SocialHub CX?

No, in SocialHub CX internal notes are never visible to citizens. Departments coordinate there and can also use templates. Only the reply that a responsible person deliberately sends goes out.

Does a department see only its own requests in SocialHub CX?

SocialHub CX gives each department its own working view of its tickets via routing, filters and folders; separate role and permission management per team is not part of the product. Folders never show more tickets than a person may see anyway, and only admins create folders and templates. Every ticket change is in the activity log.
