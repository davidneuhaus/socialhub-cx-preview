Use cases: Retail & loyalty

How service teams in retail & loyalty handle typical requests with SocialHub CX.

Working through request peaks after campaigns

A discount campaign starts. Questions about conditions and delivery times pile up under the campaign post, plus direct messages, WhatsApp messages and emails.

Your team answers campaign questions consistently, misses no request and uses the analysis to plan staffing for future campaigns.

How it works in SocialHub CX

  1. Comments, direct messages, WhatsApp messages and emails land as tickets in the workspace.
  2. A rule-based folder collects all tickets about the campaign, e.g. via tags.
  3. Templates with the campaign conditions are inserted via “/” and personalised with variables.
  4. Bulk actions mark similar tickets as done; tickets that only become relevant later are snoozed until a set time.
  5. The dashboard shows arrival times by hour and weekday – useful for planning the next campaign.

Answering reviews and comments

Reviews on Google, Trustpilot and the Play Store have so far been handled irregularly by different people. Nobody knows which review already has a reply.

Reviews are handled reliably, each has a clear owner, and sentiment is visible in the dashboard.

How it works in SocialHub CX

  1. The review platforms are connected as channels; new reviews become tickets in the Social module.
  2. Filters by channel and type show all open reviews.
  3. Tickets are assigned to people or teams; status and owner are visible to all.
  4. Templates for typical review replies ensure a consistent tone.
  5. The social dashboard shows the breakdown by channel and sentiment.

Answering questions on points, rewards and partners

Members ask why points are missing, how rewards are redeemed or which partners take part – by email, direct message and WhatsApp.

Members receive consistent replies, and your team maintains knowledge in one place – in articles and templates.

How it works in SocialHub CX

  1. All requests land as tickets in the workspace; emails are assigned to the programme team by keyword rule (“points”, “reward”).
  2. The Co-Pilot drafts replies from terms of participation and partner FAQs and shows the sources.
  3. Templates cover standard cases; help-centre articles are linked directly.
  4. Cases that need a check in the programme system are snoozed or set to “Waiting”.
  5. Feedback on the Co-Pilot goes into a review queue; wrong content is removed.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How does SocialHub CX help with many similar requests?

SocialHub CX helps with similar requests through rule-based folders, templates with variables and bulk actions. Folders group the tickets, templates ensure consistent replies, and bulk actions mark many tickets as done, assign them to you or change their priority at once.

Can SocialHub CX handle comments under campaign posts?

Yes, SocialHub CX turns comments under posts into tickets, as well as direct messages, mentions and reviews. Available channels include Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and LinkedIn, some as add-ons. Your team filters by channel, message type and date range.

Which review platforms does SocialHub CX support?

SocialHub CX supports Google Business Profile, Trustpilot and Google Play Store as channels for reviews, some as add-ons. Reviews become tickets there like comments or direct messages and can be filtered by channel and type.

Does SocialHub CX show the sentiment of reviews?

Yes, SocialHub CX shows the breakdown of incoming messages by sentiment, channel and type in the social dashboard. You see how sentiment in reviews and comments is distributed in the selected period and can view it together with response rate and response time.

Does SocialHub CX know our members' point balances?

No, SocialHub CX does not know point balances, because it includes no connection to loyalty or CRM systems. Your team checks balances in the programme system and replies in SocialHub CX. The customer history shows all previous tickets of a person.

How does SocialHub CX keep programme answers up to date?

SocialHub CX keeps answers up to date because the Co-Pilot draws on your help-centre articles, files and past conversations and prefers the article in case of conflicts. When programme terms change, you update articles and templates centrally. Replies your team rates as wrong land in a review queue.
