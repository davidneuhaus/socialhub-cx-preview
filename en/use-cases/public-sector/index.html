Use cases: Public sector

How service teams in public sector handle typical requests with SocialHub CX.

SocialHub CX covers two typical workflows in municipalities and public administration: handling citizen requests from social media and mailboxes centrally and clarifying ownership across departments. Each workflow shows step by step how requests are captured, assigned and answered.

Handling citizen requests from social media and mailboxes centrally

The city receives questions about opening hours, appointments and construction sites via Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp and the citizen mailbox. The press office handles the channels alongside daily business.

Citizens receive consistent replies, and the communications office keeps all channels in one view.

How it works in SocialHub CX

  1. Social media profiles, WhatsApp and shared mailboxes are connected; every request becomes a ticket.
  2. Frequent questions are answered with templates the communications office maintains centrally.
  3. The Co-Pilot drafts replies from stored citizen information and shows the sources.
  4. Requests a department must clarify are assigned to that department's team.
  5. The dashboard shows volume, backlog and first responses.

Clarifying responsibilities across departments

A question about a road closure concerns both the public order office and the civil engineering office. So far the email gets forwarded, and nobody knows who replies.

Every request has a clear owner, coordination stays on the ticket, and the handling path is traceable.

How it works in SocialHub CX

  1. Departments are created as teams; tickets are assigned to a team or person.
  2. Other departments follow the ticket as watchers.
  3. Coordination happens in internal notes on the ticket.
  4. If the reply depends on feedback, the ticket is set to “Waiting” or snoozed until a set time.
  5. The activity log shows every step.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can municipalities use SocialHub CX for citizen requests?

Yes, municipalities can use SocialHub CX for citizen requests from social media, WhatsApp and shared mailboxes. Requests become tickets, templates ensure consistent replies, and teams per department take over specialist questions. For procurement, the service description is available online; further documents are tailored to your request.

Can the communications office maintain templates centrally in SocialHub CX?

Yes, in SocialHub CX admins maintain templates centrally, in folders and with variables such as first name. All staff insert them via “/” in the editor. When information changes, such as opening hours, you update the template once for everyone.

Can SocialHub CX map several departments?

Yes, SocialHub CX maps departments as teams that have their own views and serve as assignment targets. Keyword rules per mailbox can assign emails directly to a department. Other participants follow tickets as watchers and are notified of changes.

Do citizens see internal coordination in SocialHub CX?

No, in SocialHub CX internal notes are never visible to citizens. Departments coordinate there and can also use templates. Only the reply that a responsible person deliberately sends goes out.
