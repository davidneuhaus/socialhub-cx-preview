Security & privacy

Security, privacy and hosting of SocialHub CX

Hosting & data location

SocialHub CX runs on the SocialHub platform. Data is stored on German servers in Falkenstein and Nuremberg and processed in our subcontractor's data centre in compliance with GDPR and BDSG on the basis of data processing agreements. Data transfer is secured via SSL/SSH, VPN tunnels and firewalls.

Certifications

SocialHub is ISO 27001 certified and undergoes regular penetration tests. Hosting is provided by a provider with a BSI C5 attestation. SocialHub itself does not hold its own C5 attestation – according to the BSI, a C5 attestation always applies to audited cloud services, not to a company as a whole.

GDPR & data processing

As a German company, SocialHub complies with the BDSG and the EU GDPR. SocialHub CX is covered by the same data protection agreements as SocialHub. A data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR is available on request.

AI & data

SocialHub CX's AI runs on Microsoft Azure with an EU endpoint. The Co-Pilot shows the sources of its answers, and a person reviews and sends every reply; prepared drafts are never sent automatically. Learning from solved tickets is off by default and, once activated, uses anonymised summaries. We do not name a specific AI model.

Access & traceability

Single sign-on (SSO) is available; accounts are managed via the SocialHub platform. Only admins change templates and folders, folders never show more tickets than a person may see, and every ticket change appears in the activity log.

Accessibility

Public bodies must require accessibility under BITV 2.0 and EN 301 549 in their specifications. SocialHub CX has been tested for accessibility according to BITV. Further accessibility information is available on request.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where is SocialHub CX hosted?

SocialHub CX is hosted in Germany (Falkenstein, Nuremberg). Data is processed in our subcontractor's data centre in compliance with GDPR and BDSG on the basis of data processing agreements. The hosting provider holds a BSI C5 attestation; data transfer is secured via SSL/SSH, VPN tunnels and firewalls.

Does SocialHub CX have its own BSI C5 attestation?

No, SocialHub CX and SocialHub do not hold their own BSI C5 attestation. Hosting is provided by a provider with a BSI C5 attestation. According to the BSI, a C5 attestation always applies to specific audited cloud services, not to a company as a whole. Further hosting information is available on request.

Is SocialHub CX ISO 27001 certified?

SocialHub CX is part of SocialHub, and SocialHub is ISO 27001 certified. The certification covers SocialHub's information security management system, not the hosting provider. In addition, SocialHub undergoes regular penetration tests. The certificate is included in the procurement & IT documents.

Is there a GDPR data processing agreement for SocialHub CX?

Yes, for SocialHub CX a data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR is available on request. The same data protection agreements apply as for the SocialHub platform. As a German company, SocialHub complies with the BDSG and the EU GDPR.

Does SocialHub CX use our customer data to train the AI?

SocialHub CX does not train AI models on customer data. The Co-Pilot only uses solved tickets if you activate “Learn from solved tickets”; it is off by default. Once activated, tickets from the last twelve months serve as anonymised summaries for suggestions in your account. Content your team repeatedly rates as wrong is removed.

Where does SocialHub CX's AI run?

SocialHub CX's AI runs on Microsoft Azure with an EU endpoint. We do not name a specific AI model. Regardless, the Co-Pilot shows the sources of its answers from your knowledge base, and a person reviews and sends every reply.

Does SocialHub CX support single sign-on?

Yes, SocialHub CX supports single sign-on (SSO). Your team signs in through your organisation's central login, and accounts are managed via the SocialHub platform. We clarify your IT's requirements for the connection in a conversation.

Is SocialHub CX accessible under BITV 2.0?

SocialHub CX has been tested for accessibility according to BITV. For public buyers who require accessibility under BITV 2.0 and EN 301 549 in their specifications, this is important evidence. Further accessibility information is available on request.

Is SocialHub CX suitable for public tenders?

SocialHub CX is suitable for tenders on social media, customer dialogue and email management, not for tenders whose focus is on other channels. For suitability and security reviews you receive the service description (available online), SocialHub's ISO 27001 certificate and further documents for data-protection, security and suitability reviews, tailored to your request.

Is European customers' data hosted in the EU or in Germany?

Is a C5 attestation enough to meet the BSI minimum standard for external cloud services?

For SocialHub CX, the C5 attestation lies with the hosting provider: SocialHub CX is hosted in Germany (Falkenstein, Nuremberg) by a provider with a BSI C5 attestation; SocialHub itself does not hold its own C5 attestation. Whether that is sufficient for your requirements under the BSI minimum standard is assessed by your authority. Which standard applies to accessible software in public administration?

SocialHub CX has been tested for accessibility according to BITV. For public bodies, accessibility under BITV 2.0 is decisive, which refers to the European standard EN 301 549. Contracting authorities therefore require it in their specifications. Further accessibility information is available on request.

Does the AI run in the EU?

SocialHub CX's AI runs on Microsoft Azure with an EU endpoint. The Co-Pilot drafts replies and summaries based on your knowledge sources and shows them; a person sends every reply. SocialHub CX does not train AI models on customer data. All other SocialHub CX data is hosted in Germany (Falkenstein, Nuremberg).
